ℹ️ 1. About Scratch AI & Detection Methodology

Scratch AI is an independent, community-driven open-source initiative designed to provide crowdsourced AI detection classifications for Scratch projects (scratch.mit.edu).

The system combines:

  • A lightweight browser extension (Chrome & Firefox) that allows users to cast classification votes directly on Scratch project pages and view real-time community verdicts.
  • A centralized, transactional backend service ensuring one vote per user per project and live statistical aggregation.
  • A public Community Web Explorer offering transparent read-only inspection and Open Data CSV downloads.

Verdict Determination Formula

Project verdicts are calculated strictly and deterministically based on real community votes:

  • Insufficient data (⏳): Fewer than 5 community votes have been cast.
  • AI-generated (🤖): At least 5 votes recorded, and AI votes represent more than 50% of the total.
  • Human-created (👤): At least 5 votes recorded, and Human votes represent more than 50% of the total.
  • Undetermined (⚖️): At least 5 votes recorded, and AI and Human votes are exactly equal (50% / 50%).

🔒 3. Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

Scratch AI operates strictly on the principle of Privacy by Design and data minimization in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR n° 2016/679) and Google Chrome Web Store Developer Privacy Requirements.

3.1. Collected Data

  • Random Anonymous Voter ID (UUID v4): On first installation, a random UUID string (e.g., ba99d8b9-a998-45fd-8f43-62f09baa79b8) is generated locally inside your browser storage (chrome.storage.local). This identifier contains zero personal identity data (no name, no email address, no password, no phone number, and no tracking cookies).
  • Classification Choice & Project ID: When you submit a vote, only the numeric Scratch project ID (e.g., 1012849182), your choice (ai or human), and the submission timestamp are stored.
  • Technical Connection Logs: Incoming HTTP requests include technical IP addresses processed solely in memory for sliding-window anti-spam rate limiting. IP addresses are not linked to your identity and are never sold or used for profiling.

3.2. Data That Is NEVER Collected

  • ❌ No personal names, real-world identities, or email addresses.
  • ❌ No Scratch account login credentials or session tokens.
  • ❌ No general browsing history outside public Scratch project pages.
  • ❌ No advertising cookies, third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel), or behavioral trackers.

3.3. Purpose and Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6.1.f)

Processing is founded on the legitimate interest of maintaining a fair, abuse-resistant crowdsourced service:

  • Preventing ballot-box stuffing and fraudulent duplicate votes via the database constraint UNIQUE(user_id, project_id).
  • Allowing voters to modify their previous choice on a project without inflating vote counts.

3.4. Data Retention & User Rights (GDPR Articles 15 to 22)

  • Right of Erasure & Reset: You can reset or delete your local UUID at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing local browser extension storage.
  • Right of Access & Inquiries: Users may request information or deletion of votes associated with their specific UUID by opening an issue on GitHub.

📜 4. Terms of Service & Community Disclaimer

4.1. Nature of Verdicts

Disclaimer of Opinion:
All verdicts and statistics displayed by Scratch AI represent strictly participatory community opinions.
They do not constitute technically verified forensic analysis, guaranteed factual claims, or official judgments regarding any Scratch project creator.

4.2. Acceptable Use Policy

Users must not:

  • Deploy automated scripts, bots, or scraping tools to artificially manipulate project statistics.
  • Attempt to bypass rate limiting or moderation controls.

The service administrators reserve the right to ban abusive UUIDs and purge fraudulent voting batches.
